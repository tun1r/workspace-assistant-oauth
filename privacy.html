Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Personal Workspace Assistant is a personal tool operated by Tunir Sarbabidya. It uses Google Workspace APIs to perform tasks requested by the account holder.

Google data accessed

Depending on the permissions granted and the task, the assistant can access and modify Gmail messages, Drive files, calendars, documents, spreadsheets, presentations, tasks and Google Classroom information. Google’s consent screen shows the permissions requested for each account.

How data is used and stored

Google data is used to carry out the account holder’s requests, such as finding messages, reviewing files or managing calendar events. Credentials are stored on the operator’s devices. Task data and results may be retained in agent session records, logs or files on those devices. Retention depends on the tool and its configuration.

When an AI agent processes a request, relevant content and task context may be sent to the configured AI service providers to generate a response. Those providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies. The operator should avoid requesting access to information they do not want sent to the selected provider.

Access and deletion

Account holders can revoke access at Google Account connections. To request deletion of locally stored credentials or data, contact tunir.sarbabidya@gmail.com. Revoking access does not automatically delete copies already saved in session records or files.

Google API data

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
